The Dreamer gives any local model a sleep cycle: it ingests a day of raw memories, then reorganizes them overnight into typed connections — merging duplicates, forming generalizations, materializing transitive links, resolving contradictions by evidence, and pruning noise — so the next morning it answers questions it could not answer before, with no retraining. A live demo lets you run the night and quiz the raw versus dreamed memory side by side.

Sovereign bolt-on. Pure kernel, zero-dep, offline — it dreams on your machine, no cloud, no retraining. Model-agnostic: feed it any local model's day of memories (Ollama, BYOK, or your own store), get back a consolidated memory + retrieve. Gate 27/27.
Honest scope. This is knowledge consolidation, not gradient learning — no weights are touched. It doesn't invent facts: generalizations are recombinations of what was seen, kept as hypotheses with provenance, and a specific counter-fact always wins (a penguin is a bird, but it still doesn't fly). It sharpens the model's context, not its parameters. Gary's method, generalized. Built on the-kg + seedmind. Code: github.com/sjgant80-hub/the-dreamer.
